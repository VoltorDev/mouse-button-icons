# Changelog

## 1.2.1 (05-11-2026)

- The addon list in game now shows its English name, **Mouse Button Icons** (the folder is still `IconosRaton`, so your settings are kept). No other changes.

## 1.2.0 (03-11-2026)

- F10, F11 and F12 are drawn as the G6, G7 and G8 buttons of a Logitech G502 only with the new option
  «F10, F11 and F12 as G6, G7 and G8» (off by default). Whoever already had saved options from 1.1.0 keeps it on.
- Options in English, and in Spanish with an esES or esMX client.
- Turning that option off removes the icon at once instead of waiting for Blizzard to repaint the bar.

## 1.1.0

- Options menu: turn the icons on or off and choose their size (60-160 %).

## 1.0.0

- Mouse icon instead of "Mouse Button 3/4/5" on the keybinds of the eight Blizzard action bars.
